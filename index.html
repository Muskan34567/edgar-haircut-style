What Is an Edgar Low Taper Fade for Outfit?

An Edgar low taper fade combines the recognizable Edgar haircut with a gradual taper around the sides and back. Instead of removing too much hair near the temples, a low taper keeps the transition relatively subtle while allowing the top and front to remain the main feature.

This makes the haircut particularly useful for outfit styling because it can work with both casual and fashionable clothing. The structured front adds definition, while the low taper gives the overall appearance a cleaner finish.

25 Edgar Low Taper Fade Outfit Ideas to Try

1. Classic Edgar Streetwear

Classic streetwear is one of the easiest ways to style an Edgar haircut. Try a relaxed graphic tee, straight or baggy jeans, and low-profile sneakers. Keep the colors simple if your haircut has strong texture.

2. Fluffy Edgar Casual Style

A fluffy Edgar has more volume and movement on top. Because the hair already creates texture, pair it with relaxed clothing rather than overly complicated layers like a plain oversized tee and straight-leg trousers.

3. Edgar Takuache Look

The Edgar Takuache look has strong roots in Mexican-inspired youth style and often combines a defined haircut with casual streetwear, graphic tees, and comfortable sneakers.

Pro Styling Tip: If your haircut is heavily textured, your outfit does not need to be equally complicated. Start with one main piece (like an oversized hoodie or varsity jacket) and keep the rest clean and balanced.

FAQs: Edgar Low Taper Fade Outfit Styles

What Goes With an Edgar Haircut?

Edgar haircuts work wonderfully with tees, hoodies, jeans, cargos, sneakers, varsity jackets, and bomber jackets.

How Do You Style a Fluffy Edgar?

Keep the top textured with natural volume. Pair it with relaxed pieces like oversized tees, hoodies, and straight jeans to complement the fuller shape.
